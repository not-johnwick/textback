# What to ask a new customer

<!-- The assistant asks these before promising anything. Never more than two questions per text. -->

Always:
1. Safety first for anything gas: any gas smell, or a CO alarm going off?
2. Address or nearest major intersection.
3. Is the system still running at all?

Then, by job:
- No heat: brand and rough age of the furnace; any error light blinking; anyone vulnerable at home (baby, elderly, medical).
- Strange noise: when does it happen (startup, running, shutdown); how long has it been going on.
- New install / quote: house size, current system age, best time for a visit.
- Ask for a photo of the label inside the furnace door (brand, model, serial). It saves a trip.
- Tenant or owner? If tenant, who is the landlord and who pays.
